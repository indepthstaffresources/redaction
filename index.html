The saved PDF is made of images. That's what makes the redaction real: the original text no longer exists in the file. It won't be searchable, and it will be larger than the original.
Read it before you send it. The tool catches patterns. It will miss identifiers in unusual formats, can't read scanned pages with no text layer, and can't recognise names unless you list them. The person sending the document is responsible for checking it.
